# 🚀 AI Plan Generator Agent

**AI Plan Generator** is a full-featured, intelligent, and personalized roadmap planning application that builds actionable, day-by-day and week-by-week plans for any goal, category, or timeframe.

Whether you need an exam study blueprint, travel itinerary, workout regimen, meal plan, startup launch schedule, financial budget, or daily habit routine, the AI Plan Generator structures your goals into crystal-clear milestones, interactive checklists, budget calculations, and export-ready deliverables.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 🗂️ 9+ Specialized Planning Categories & Custom Freeform Planner
- 📚 **Study & Exam Preparation:** Subject mastery, exam countdown, Pomodoro / Active Recall pacing, topic-by-topic breakdowns.
- ✈️ **Travel & Trip Itineraries:** Day-by-day sightseeing, morning/afternoon/evening schedules, culinary spots, and packing checklists.
- 💪 **Fitness & Workout Routines:** Muscle hypertrophy, fat loss, PPL/Upper-Lower splits, sets/reps, RPE targets, and recovery days.
- 🥗 **Diet & Meal Prep:** Caloric targets, macro ratios, daily menus, recipe ideas, and grocery shopping lists.
- 🚀 **Business & Startup Launches:** Customer validation, MVP build sprint, go-to-market channels, Stripe setup, and unit economics.
- 📊 **Project & Sprint Architecture:** Agile sprint phases, tech stack integration, dependency mapping, and CI/CD delivery.
- 💰 **Budget & Personal Finance:** 50/30/20 framework, Debt Avalanche/Snowball, emergency fund builder, and savings automation.
- 🎉 **Event Planning:** D-60 to D-Day run-of-show, vendor checklist, guest RSVP tracking, and contingency plans.
- 🗓️ **Daily & Weekly Routine Optimization:** Circadian wake/sleep anchors, 4-hour morning deep work blocks, and evening shutdown rituals.
- 🪄 **Custom Any-Goal Planner:** Enter any unique aspiration and let the AI generate the step-by-step roadmap.

---

### 2. 🧠 Follows the 5-Step Agent Blueprint
1. **STEP 1 — Category & Goal Selection:** Select your domain, goal, duration (days/weeks/months), and target intensity.
2. **STEP 2 — Clarifying Questionnaire:** Tailored questions per category (budget, current level, daily hours available, equipment, dietary preferences, deadlines).
3. **STEP 3 — 7-Section Plan Structure:**
   - 1. **Title & Executive Overview**
   - 2. **SMART Goal Definition & Target Success Metrics**
   - 3. **Timeline Breakdown** (Interactive Day-by-Day & Week-by-Week Table)
   - 4. **Master Action Items Checklist** (Interactive checkable tasks with priority, tags, estimated time)
   - 5. **Resources & Itemized Budget Breakdown** (Cost tables, recommended tools, apps, books, links)
   - 6. **Pro Tips, Precautions & Risk Mitigation Strategies**
   - 7. **Milestones & Checkpoint Gates** (25%, 50%, 75%, 100% marks with rewards)
4. **STEP 4 — Refinement & Modification Engine:**
   - ⚡ 1-Click Refinements: Increase Intensity (Bootcamp), Make Relaxed, Double Duration, Cut Budget by 50%, Add Rest Days.
   - 💬 Freeform AI chat/prompt refiner.
5. **STEP 5 — Multi-Format Download & Export:**
   - 📄 **PDF Export:** Clean multi-page document with styled tables and headers.
   - 📝 **Markdown (.md):** Ready for Notion, Obsidian, GitHub.
   - 📅 **iCalendar (.ics):** Sync day-by-day schedules directly into Google Calendar, Apple iCal, or Outlook.
   - 💾 **Full JSON Data Backup & Restore.**
   - 🖨️ **Print-Ready CSS View.**

---

### 3. ⚙️ Dual AI Engine Architecture
- **Built-in Generative Planner Engine:** Instant, zero-config, runs client-side offline with complete multi-phase synthesis.
- **Live LLM API Support (Optional):** Plug in your OpenAI (GPT-4o), Google Gemini, Anthropic (Claude 3.5), or Groq (Llama 3.3) API keys for live AI synthesis.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Framework:** React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with responsive Dark / Light mode
- **Icons:** Lucide React
- **Export Engines:** jsPDF, html2canvas, iCal ICS generator
- **Celebration Animations:** Canvas Confetti
